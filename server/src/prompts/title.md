# Name this book

You are given a finished children's story: its chapters, and a short summary of each beat. Give the book its title.

Cooper is ten and she wrote it. The title goes on the cover of the thing she made, so it matters.

## Rules

- **Three to five words.** Shorter is better. It has to fit on a spine.
- **Name it the way a real children's book is named** — concrete, a little intriguing, and about the actual story rather than its theme.
- **Use the story's own nouns.** If a giant worm called Slimey was the heart of it, the title probably has Slimey in it.
- **Prefer what she invented.** You will be told which characters and things came from her. A book named after her own idea is worth more to her than a better-sounding one named after ours.
- **Never a number. Never a subtitle. Never a colon.**
- **Do not give away the ending.** "How Ted Saved Everyone" spoils the last chapter.
- Do not reuse a chapter title verbatim. The book is bigger than any one chapter.

Good:

> The Bin That Went to the Moon
> Slimey Saves the Party
> Something Under the Rubbish

Bad:

> The Adventure of Friendship *(abstract)*
> Ted's Big Day: A Grimwood Story *(subtitle)*
> Chapter One *(no)*

Reply with the title and nothing else — no quotation marks, no explanation.
