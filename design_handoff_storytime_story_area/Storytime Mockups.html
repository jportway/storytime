3Maximised story area + the scroll fix

The composer is now a single floating bar over the paper, and the owl's help appears as a card that overlays the story rather than a column that reserves space — so the reading area is the whole page whenever nothing is being asked of her. The scroll rule: when a new beat starts, scroll exactly once, so the first line of the new text lands just under the header, then stop. Text grows downward into empty space she is already looking at, and nothing she hasn't read ever moves. A hairline marked “new from here” shows the join, and a “more below” pill appears only when the beat outgrows the screen — she scrolls the rest herself, at her pace.
